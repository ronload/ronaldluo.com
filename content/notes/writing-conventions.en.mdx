Every note here is written in Traditional Chinese first, then translated into English. Both languages share one slug and are distinguished only by the URL prefix.

## File naming

One file per language per post, under `content/notes/`:

```text
<slug>.zh-TW.mdx
<slug>.en.mdx
```

The slug must be kebab-case ASCII with no dots. The English file may be missing, in which case the English route serves the Chinese body and is marked as non-indexable.

## Frontmatter

```yaml
title: Title
description: Summary, used in the feed and in search results
date: 2026-07-26
tags:
  - engineering
draft: false
```

## Three Chinese typesetting traps

First, use `**` for bold, never `__`. Underscore syntax adjacent to Han characters is not parsed by CommonMark, and no plugin fixes that case.

Second, native CommonMark also fails when an emphasis span begins or ends with full-width punctuation. This site installs `remark-cjk-friendly`, so those cases render correctly.

Third, a soft line break inside a paragraph leaves a visible space between Han characters. `remark-join-cjk-lines` joins those lines, while keeping the space at a boundary between Chinese and Latin text, where a space genuinely belongs.
